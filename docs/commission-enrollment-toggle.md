# Platform Admin commission enrollment control

8 November 2026. Platform Admin Settings → General now contains a fixed **10%** commission enrollment switch, current status, activation time in PHT, reason and confirmation fields, a confirmation dialog, and paginated activation history. The legacy editable rate field was removed from this form; attempts to submit a rate are rejected, and historical settings/commission snapshots are not rewritten.

Only a currently approved Platform Admin who is not a Logistics account may change enrollment. The service refreshes and locks the actor, validates the expected settings revision and requires explicit confirmation. Activation additionally requires a valid active Platform receiving destination. The Admin must attest that official account ownership and operational activation requirements have been verified; the software cannot independently verify GCash ownership. Adding the switch does not clear the security/recovery blockers in the live activation review.

`commission_enrollment_settings` is the authoritative singleton after installation. Its default is disabled, activation is null and revision is 1. Website requests never write `.env`, change the application key or refresh configuration caches. Cached environment flags cannot override installed database settings. For deployments without this migration, the prior configuration policy remains a compatibility fallback; the new control is unavailable until installation. A missing singleton fails closed for enrollment.

Activation sets a server-generated cutoff at the **next whole second**, matching the precision of order and purchase timestamps. Orders created earlier in the activation second cannot accidentally become chargeable. No client-supplied cutoff, backdating or rate is accepted. New COD orders and new GCash purchases at or after the cutoff may enroll; a GCash purchase made before the cutoff remains grandfathered when its order is placed later.

Deactivation stops new snapshots only. It preserves the last activation timestamp and all obligations, earning/reconciliation, manual payment and actual-receipt verification workflows. Reactivation sets a new future cutoff, so orders from the disabled interval cannot be backfilled. Previously enrolled GCash purchases can still link to later orders. Enrollment capture and policy changes share a database row lock; concurrent/stale requests cannot silently overwrite status. Unique audit revisions and a transaction bind each settings change to its audit event. Audit rows record actor identity, action, reason, previous enabled/cutoff values, resulting cutoff, revision and server time. No audit edit/delete endpoint is provided; linked actors use restrictive deletion and destructive migration rollback is refused.
